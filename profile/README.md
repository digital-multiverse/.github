### 🌱 Echoes of the Garden
An idle creature collector coming to Steam — every creature has a real life cycle (born, grown,
aged, gone), with permadeath, an inheritance system, and a player-driven economy.
→ **[echoesofthegarden.com](https://echoesofthegarden.com)**

### 🗳️ comparaVoto
A neutral civic site for Spain's general election of 29 November 2026: party manifestos compared
topic by topic, every result since 1977, legislatures and their governments. Every figure links to
its official source. (Spanish)
→ **[comparavoto.es](https://comparavoto.es)**
