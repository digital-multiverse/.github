# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Digital Multiverse to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to **conduct@digital-multiverse.dev**.
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or ban participants who violate this code.
